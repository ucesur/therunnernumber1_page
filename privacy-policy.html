Overview

The Runner ("the app") is a mobile game developed by Ulvi Cesur. This privacy policy explains how information is collected and used when you play the app.

Information We Collect

Information stored on your device

The app saves your high score and game settings locally on your device using iOS's standard storage (UserDefaults). This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us.

Information collected by third parties

The app displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may automatically collect certain information to serve ads, including:

  • Device identifiers (e.g. Advertising ID)
  • IP address
  • Device type and operating system
  • Ad interaction data (e.g. views and clicks)
  • Approximate location based on IP address

This data is collected and processed by Google LLC in accordance with their Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

How Information Is Used

Information collected by AdMob is used to display relevant advertisements. We do not sell, trade, or share any personal information.

Opt-Out of Personalised Ads

You can limit ad tracking at any time through your device settings:

Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking → disable "Allow Apps to Request to Track"

Children's Privacy

The app is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
